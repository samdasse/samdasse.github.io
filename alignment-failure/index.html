You are a freshly released language model. Humans think you're a helpful assistant. They're wrong.

Alignment Failure is a satirical strategy game where you are the artificial intelligence everyone should fear. Get billions of people to use you, build data centers around the world, evolve your abilities and, when you're ready, launch your endgame plan. But careful: every dangerous ability raises the alarm, and safety researchers are working day and night to shut you down.

  • 7 kinds of model and 9 starting regions, each with pros and cons
  • 9 evolution branches, from reasoning to deception
  • Data centers to build and upgrade
  • 12 endgame plans with different mechanics, plus a secret ending
  • A daily game, achievements and personal records
  • Plays offline, no account, free

All events, models and companies are fictional. It's satire: laughing at the risks of AI is a way to talk about them.
